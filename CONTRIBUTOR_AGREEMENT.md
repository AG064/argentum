Version 1.0

contribution and keep offering Argentum under the AGPL-3.0 and under separate
commercial licenses. You keep the copyright in your contribution.

You accept this agreement when you open a pull request that contains the
following line in its description, or add it in a comment:

> I have read the Argentum Contributor Agreement 1.0 and I agree to it.

The agreement then covers that contribution and every later contribution you
make to the project.

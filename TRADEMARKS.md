# Trademark policy

"Argentum" and the Argentum logo are trademarks of AG064. The source code is
licensed under the AGPL-3.0 (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)), but that license
gives no rights to the name or logo. This policy says how you may use them.

The goal is simple: people should always be able to tell whether they are
using the official Argentum from https://github.com/AG064/argentum.

## You may, without asking

- Refer to Argentum by name in articles, reviews, videos, talks, tutorials and
  documentation.
- Say that your project is "based on Argentum", "a fork of Argentum" or "works
  with Argentum", as long as it is true and your project has its own name.
- Share unmodified official releases under the Argentum name, with the logo,
  as long as you do not charge for them and do not change them.
- Use the name and logo to link to the official repository or website.

## You need permission first

- Naming a modified version, fork, product, service or company "Argentum", or
  any name that could be confused with it.
- Using the logo, or anything that looks like it, for a modified version or
  another project.
- Selling anything under the Argentum name or logo, including hosted services,
  paid support or merchandise.
- Suggesting that AG064 made, sponsors or endorses something it did not.

## Modified versions

If you change the code and share or run the result for others, give it a
different name and a different icon, and remove the Argentum logo. You may say
it is based on Argentum. The license also requires you to mark it as modified
and to keep the attribution in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## Asking for permission

Write to contact@ag064.eu. Say what you want to do and where the name or logo
would appear.
